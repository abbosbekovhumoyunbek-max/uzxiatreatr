# UZXIAtreatr — Product Strategy & Specification

## 1. Product Vision
**UZXIAtreatr** is an advanced digital academy platform and technology/media ecosystem designed for an innovative university/academy environment. It fuses modern artificial intelligence education, interactive learning, rich multimedia storytelling, student life showcase, and academy news into a unified, high-performance web experience.

The platform embodies the theme: **"Kelajak texnologiyalari va intellektual bilimlar makoni"** (The Realm of Future Technologies and Intellectual Knowledge).

---

## 2. Target Audience & Personas
- **Students & Applicants**: Young, tech-savvy individuals seeking knowledge in AI, programming, and modern technologies.
- **Instructors & Researchers**: Educators displaying course modules, research projects, and academic news.
- **Academy Visitors & Partners**: Industry leaders and prospective students exploring academy life, achievements, and events.
- **AI Enthusiasts**: Learners accessing daily AI facts, interactive quizzes, and educational tools.

---

## 3. Core Value Propositions
1. **Interactive AI Learning**: Mini-courses, daily facts, interactive quizzes, and prompt engineering tutorials.
2. **Rich Multimedia Center**: High-definition video showcase, event photo galleries with lightboxes, and project spotlights.
3. **Dynamic Academy Life**: Up-to-date news feed, scheduled events calendar, and student resource hub.
4. **World-Class UI/UX**: Dark/light theme design tokens, glassmorphism, responsive architecture, and accessible micro-interactions.

---

## 4. Content Strategy & Localization
- **Primary UI Language**: Uzbek (`uz-UZ` / `uz-Latn`).
- **Future i18n Readiness**: English (`en`) and Russian (`ru`) dictionary keys structure built into data layer.
- **Voice & Tone**: Intelligent, modern, encouraging, professional, and youthful.

---

## 5. Success Metrics (KPIs)
- **User Engagement**: Quiz completion rates, interactive fact interactions, video views.
- **Performance**: Lighthouse score > 90 across Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO.
- **Usability**: 100% keyboard nav support, smooth mobile navigation, < 1.5s First Contentful Paint.
